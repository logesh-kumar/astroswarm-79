Privacy Policy — AstroSwarm 79

Version: AstroSwarm 79 v2.0.0
Effective Date: November 21, 2026
Developer: Cubbing Solutions Pvt Ltd

1. Offline Mode (Zero Tracking & Zero Ads)

AstroSwarm 79 can be played completely in Offline Mode without creating an account or connecting to the internet. In Offline Mode, the game works completely offline with zero advertisements, zero third-party ad tracking, and zero behavioral analytics. Campaign progress and local high scores remain solely on your device unless you opt into cloud features.

2. Optional Cloud Accounts, Leaderboards & Score Sharing

AstroSwarm 79 v2.0.0 offers optional online features powered by Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (hosted under our Google Cloud / Firebase project cubbing-games-hub).

When a player chooses to sign in (via Guest, Email/Password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In) to save cloud progress or post leaderboard scores, we collect and store only the data required to operate those features:

  • Firebase User ID (uid): A unique account identifier generated by Firebase Authentication.
  • Pilot Callsign / Display Name: The chosen pilot callsign shown on your profile and public leaderboards.
  • Email Address: Collected only if provided when using Email/Password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple (not collected for Guest accounts).
  • Gameplay & Leaderboard Data: Campaign stars, unlocked stages/skins, and submitted leaderboard scores.

All cloud account and leaderboard data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS and is never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Score Card Image Sharing

When you choose to share a score card image, AstroSwarm 79 renders the image into temporary app storage and opens your device's OS native Share Sheet only when you explicitly tap Share. No images or screen contents are captured or uploaded automatically.

4. Third-Party Services & Ads

AstroSwarm 79 contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no ad networks, and no cross-app tracking. Authentication and cloud storage rely solely on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore under cubbing-games-hub), Apple Sign-In, and Google Sign-In when initiated by the player.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your AstroSwarm 79 cloud account and associated data at any time using either method below:

In-App Account Deletion (Immediate)

Open Pilot Account -> Delete Account & Data -> Confirm Permanent Delete inside AstroSwarm 79 to permanently delete your Firebase Auth user, Cloud Firestore profile, and leaderboard entries immediately.

Web / Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app, email logesh@cubbingsolutions.com with your Pilot Callsign or Sign-In email address to request manual account and data deletion. Verified requests are processed and permanently deleted within 7 days.

6. Children's Privacy

AstroSwarm 79 contains no advertisements and performs no ad tracking. Offline Mode requires no account or personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable minimum age without parental consent; if you believe a child has created an optional cloud account without consent, please delete it in-app or contact us at logesh@cubbingsolutions.com for immediate removal.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date and version note.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact Cubbing Solutions Pvt Ltd at logesh@cubbingsolutions.com.
